What this instance stores

  • Poll responses: the name you enter, an optional email address, and your availability per date — visible to the poll owner and other respondents, kept until the poll is deleted.
  • Invitations: invitee email addresses and a log of outbound mails (invite/reminder/decision), visible to the poll owner.
  • Account data (poll owners): identity provided by the operator's sign-in system.

Cookies

Only strictly-necessary cookies are used — sign-in session, CSRF protection, and an optional signed token so you can edit your own response later (180 days). A local browser preference stores your dark/light theme choice. No tracking and no analytics, and no third-party cookie is set unless you press a button that says so — which is why no consent banner is required.

Your rights

To access, correct, or delete your data, contact the controller above. Respondents can edit their response at any time via their poll link while the poll is open.
